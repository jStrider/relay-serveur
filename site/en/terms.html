3. The service

Relay reads your Apple Health data, encrypts it on your iPhone and uploads it to a relay. Your AI agent fetches and decrypts it with the relay-agent client. In the other direction, your agent uploads running workouts that the app schedules on your Apple Watch, and it can ask the iPhone for an immediate sync.

You need an iPhone on iOS 18 or later, an Apple Watch for workouts, and an agent you run yourself. The developer provides no agent and no AI model, and is not responsible for what your agent does with your data or for the workouts it suggests.

4. No named account

Relay asks for no email and no password. Your access lives in the pairing phrase, kept in your iPhone's keychain and on your agent's machine. Keep it secret: anyone who holds it can read your data. The developer can neither recover nor reset it. If you lose it, regenerate it in the app and link your agent again; your Health data stays on your iPhone.
