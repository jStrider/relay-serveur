Privacy policy

Version of 23 October 2026. If this translation and the French version differ, the French version prevails.

Your health data is encrypted on your iPhone with a key that never leaves it. The relay stores it without being able to read it, and so does the developer.

There is no named account, no email, no advertising, no analytics and no cookies.

You delete everything the relay keeps from the app: Settings, "Delete my account".

2. What this policy covers

It covers the Relay iPhone app, the relay hosted by the developer at relay.relay-agent.app, and this website. It does not cover your AI agent or the machine running relay-agent: you run those yourself (see section 9).

If you use a relay you host yourself, the developer receives none of your data. Only a possible in-app purchase and this policy for the app still apply.

3. Your health data

With the permission you grant in iOS, the app reads from Apple Health:

  • steps, active and resting energy, exercise minutes;
  • dietary energy, protein, carbohydrates, fat and water;
  • weight and height;
  • sleep stages, resting heart rate, heart rate variability;
  • workouts, with heart rate during the workout and, for runs, walks, hikes and rides, the GPS route.

This is data concerning health, a special category under Article 9 of the GDPR. GPS routes also reveal places.

The app encrypts it on your iPhone with AES-256-GCM before sending anything. The 256-bit key is generated on the iPhone, kept in its keychain, never synced to iCloud and never sent to the relay. Only the app and the agents you have linked know it.

The server also processes:

  • daily counters: number of active accounts and syncs, with no identifier.

5. What the developer cannot read

The content of the batches, the key and the pairing phrase. Each batch is also bound to its name and to your account: moved or renamed, it no longer decrypts. If an authority demanded your data, the developer could only hand over what is marked "Yes" in the table above.

8. Who receives data

  • OVHcloud (OVH SAS, Roubaix, France) hosts the relay on a server in France and its backups in object storage in Paris. It is a processor under Article 28 of the GDPR. It only receives what the relay stores: health batches stay encrypted.
  • Apple delivers the notifications: the relay sends it your iPhone's token and either an empty silent notification or a message from your agent, encrypted by the agent, which Apple cannot read. The relay does not keep these messages; it only records when they were sent, to cap them at 30 per hour. Apple also handles the subscription purchase and payment, as controller of its own processing, under its own privacy policy. The relay only receives the transaction signed by Apple, which it verifies locally.

The relay transfers no data outside the European Union. Apple may process the data it handles itself outside the Union, under its own commitments.

9. On your agent's side

The relay-agent client decrypts your data on the machine you choose, with the pairing phrase. From then on, the data is your responsibility: the developer has no access to it. If your agent sends it to its model provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or another), that provider processes it under its own terms.

The pairing phrase gives access to all your data. Keep it in a file only you can read: relay-agent refuses to start if other users on the machine can read it. Never give it to support.

10. Security

  • AES-256-GCM end-to-end encryption; transport also goes over HTTPS.
  • Access token kept as a SHA-256 hash; quotas and rate limits per account.
  • Server reachable over SSH by key only, automatic security updates, service isolated from the rest of the system.
  • Public relay code, so anyone can check the above.

11. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and you can set instructions for what happens to your data after your death.

  • Erasure: in the app, Settings, "Delete my account". The relay immediately erases the account, all its batches, wake-up and message times, notification token and subscription status. Backups follow within the periods in section 7. Your data stays in Health, on your iPhone.
  • Regenerating the phrase erases the old account from the relay and creates a new one, with a new key.
  • Access and portability: your health data is in Health on your iPhone, and relay-agent sync writes it in clear on your machine. For the technical data in section 4, email support. We will ask you to prove the account is yours, never for your phrase.
  • Subscription: you manage and cancel it in your Apple account settings. Deleting your Relay account does not cancel it.

Email support@relay-agent.app. Reply within one month at most. If you think your rights are not respected, you can complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority: www.cnil.fr, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France, or to the authority of your country.
